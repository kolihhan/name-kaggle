**The 12-target conditional BA is NOT comparable to the official 0.679 Public 18-class BA.** It is neither an improved Kaggle score nor a claim about private leaderboard transfer. ## Global research bottleneck and decision

**Observed:** Current incumbent errors are dominated by **within-provider sibling discrimination** (71/87 errors). The strongest repeated failure is Gemini 3.8 Flash confused with 3.7/3.6 Flash; GPT 5.6 Sol/Terra exhibit substantial sibling confusion. The existing 18-way champion is highly accurate on some classes (Claude Opus 5 96.55%, GPT Astra 100%) on these sources.

**Unknown:** Whether these errors reflect actual fine-grained generator fingerprint ambiguity or the Kaggle Model Proxy serving versions and sampling/collection conditions. The two source corpora share the same 12 available labels and lack complete panels for 6 models. They cannot prove new 18-class accuracy.

**Next mechanism hypothesis:** Is there a *source-stable, prompt-conditioned* sibling identity signal that provides **new independent information beyond the incumbent**? Candidate methods worth testing without fitting a new supervised readout: pretrained base-model likelihood/activation-based contrast **within a frozen family candidate set**, or a demonstrably independent pretrained model fingerprint. Crucially, only promote if same-row independent predictions show positive rescued-minus-regressed counts on Gemini/GPT sibling errors **in both sources**, not merely another blend weight.

**Next discriminating experiment:** before touching a new Kaggle notebook, inspect existing per-row prediction/output artifacts (private Kaggle), source/run identity and prompt provenance for overlap. Identify one accessible *non-incumbent prefit* method that can make same-row predictions on both source cohorts using zero new training. Compare errors and rescue/regression vs the now-fixed 347-row champion. If the candidate requires unavailable weights, training, or uses the same provider-identity shortcut, reject it. Do not rerun incumbent, retune on this inspected diagnostic set, or manufacture fresh validation folds.
