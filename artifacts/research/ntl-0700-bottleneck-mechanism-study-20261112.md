# Name That LLM — why BA stalls at 0.679 and what would credibly reach 0.700

Research PIC study, 2026-11-12 MYT. No new model training, public competition submission, or GPU execution was authorized by this document. All numerical claims are tied to the repository's existing authentic runs or clearly identified outside sources.

## Executive finding

**The quality bottleneck is NOT the recently repaired CUDA failure.** CUDA reliability and >0.700 18-class attribution are different engineering and research questions. Fixing the former allows a model to run; it does not improve the accuracy of its underlying classifier. The minimum required verified gain is **0.021 balanced-accuracy points** (2.1 percentage points), not 0.021 in ordinary micro accuracy.

Our account's latest authenticated completed best at this checkpoint is **0.679** (Kaggle official refs `58210835` and `58233227`, tied). We also have unscored hidden rerun failures `58244483` and `58268344`; one Lightning entry `58167751` remains PENDING. The last authenticated quota was four official submissions. Source: [authenticated ledger #43219811855](https://github.com/kolihhan/name-kaggle/actions/runs/43219811855). **No score >=0.700 belongs to our account.**

A verified [official public leaderboard top-20 snapshot (Nov 11 UTC)](ntl-public-leaderboard-top20-20261112.md) had #1 **0.765** and #20 **0.727**. Thus 0.700 is demonstrably attainable in the competition but is below that snapshot's top-20 cutoff. That page does not reveal the leaders' code, nor prove their techniques transfer to our account. The official [evaluation definition](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/name-that-llm/overview/abstract) is balanced accuracy, average recall across 18 exact generating-model labels; Public uses ~40% of the hidden data, and the final Private set uses the other ~60%.

## Why the just-repaired Native candidate is NOT automatically the best 0.700 candidate

The native source was a published third-party **attributed reproduction**, with exact original source hash and six model assets but **unverified author score**; [frozen contract](../candidates/native-frozen-nov12-guarded/CONTRACT.md). Its original `q14v5.gauss=0.7` and `q8v5.gauss=0.44` would trigger supervised Gaussian fitting; the user's current no-new-training boundary requires those two flags to be changed to **0**, with guards retained. [Source fit reachability #43218217077](https://github.com/kolihhan/name-kaggle/actions/runs/43218217077). Hence our runnable candidate is **not exactly the author's scoring algorithm**. Disabling trained heads may harm exact-sibling discrimination even if everything executes perfectly. This is a hypothesis; no measured effect magnitude is established.

Treat it as a **runtime candidate**, not the quality champion. Do not spend one of the four scarce official competition submissions on it just because its 3-row private preview now runs.

## Quantifying the BA jump

BA = (1/18) sum of 18 per-label recalls. Reaching 0.700 from 0.679 requires sum(recall improvements across all 18) >= **0.378**. If focused on three weak sibling labels and all other labels were perfectly preserved, the gain needed is roughly **+12.6 percentage points recall per one of those three classes**. Actual class counts and Public/Private mixes are nonuniform, so do not convert this into a fixed number of extra correct hidden rows. Any regressions in other classes increase the required sibling gains.

## Prioritized mechanism search — choose an actual new signal, not another patch

### A. Highest priority under current NO-NEW-TRAINING boundary: source-robust pretrained sibling expert

1. Identify a **fully pretrained, inference-only** source of *new* evidence (e.g. frozen provider-sensitive encoder representation, independently trained exact-sibling readout, or genuinely independent surrogate likelihood). Preserve rights and model/source provenance. A model's public Notebook title, vote count or claimed score is **not proof** of performance on our account.
2. Evaluate only **conditionally**, where the frozen champion's top two candidates fall within the same family, and only where that independent expert is actually source-complementary. Keep the champion output untouched otherwise; never blindly rebalance all 18 classes.
3. Pair incumbent and expert predictions on a newly source-qualified 18-label test on **identical rows** and compute macro recall by source/model; errors rescued vs correct predictions damaged; prompt-cluster bootstrap; performance on 18 classes including the six missing from our 347-row diagnostic; no source-sensitive heuristic.
4. Pre-register **promotion gate** before seeing results: evidence of >=+0.03 BA on the independent paired target as a safety margin over the desired +0.021 Public gap, nonnegative change across qualified independent source groups, and credible uncertainty evidence. This threshold is a conservative *decision rule*, not a prediction. If qualified full-18 holdout cannot be acquired, report INSUFFICIENT DATA rather than optimizing the Kaggle Public board.
5. Require inference-runtime feasibility and intact frozen guard on hidden 4,000 rows before a **single** official Code submission.

The key is **conditional independent information** about confusing Gemini/GPT siblings, not more copies of the same broad family/style model or superficial blending.

### B. Best longer-term mechanism if new supervised fitting is *separately permitted* later

A same-capacity prompt-grouped **class-balanced supervised CE** baseline versus **CE + within-family, same-prompt hard-negative contrastive/ranking objective** using genuine matched prompts and source/version-verified samples. Include a **shuffled-pair control**, source-disjoint prompt holdout and original frozen champion scored on matching rows. Run no training in this research turn. Do not use PPO/GRPO: one-action closed-set known-label classification gives denser and easier-to-evaluate supervision with CE/contrastive objectives. [PPO decision](ppo-vs-task-aligned-objective-decision-20261112.md). Avoid treating a higher reused OOF score as proof of hidden transfer.

This avenue might have a higher learning ceiling than inference-only borrowing, but it violates the current *no-new-training* constraint unless explicitly reopened; it also requires a clean validation protocol.

### C. Deprioritize unless new contrary evidence arrives

- Global Sinkhorn/assignment/prior-strength tuning, per-prompt label quota, copied 0.683/0.684 Notebook names, source-dependent passage-length thresholds, and arbitrary ensemble alpha sweeps.
- Short-TRACE direct submission (own verified **0.093**), same-family public clone stacking without proven independent errors.
- PPO/GRPO for single-label classification, or adding huge GPU ensembles when input-scale feasibility is unresolved.
- Re-evaluating the already-consumed 347 passage diagnostics to select a tuned threshold; that would manufacture confirmation from spent data.

1. **DONE now** — Confirmed valid 0.679 champion, no new scored improvement; fixed legacy CPU kernel metadata; proved 3-row true full-model output; measured important unknown runtime risk.
2. **Next engineering gate** — private intermediate-row runtime benchmark of the existing frozen inference-only candidate, with per-stage timing, 9h projection and zero training. No official submission.
3. **Next research gate** — provenance/served-version and rights audit of a potential fresh 18-label dataset. Compare the incumbent to at most two materially different frozen sibling experts, not many coefficient variants. If only 12 labels can be audited, use them for exploratory error mechanism, **never for full 18-label BA**.
4. **Official promotion** — single idempotent Kaggle submission only after authentic fullpath and scale gates plus qualified transfer evidence, then verify Kaggle terminal Public BA numerically. Keep the existing 0.679 submission as the fallback.
5. **If the evidence gate cannot be met** — explain the blocker and either pursue a separately authorized discriminative training lane or locate a genuinely independent legally usable prefit model; do not call a public Notebook title or a CI success “best.”

## Bottom line

**0.700 is a legitimate attainable milestone, not yet an established outcome.** On our evidence, the highest-value remaining weakness is *exact-sibling identification that survives collection protocol shifts*; the technical run fixes merely restore experimental validity. The next bet should target this residual error pattern while keeping the proven 0.679 champion and refusing Public-LB score-shopping.
