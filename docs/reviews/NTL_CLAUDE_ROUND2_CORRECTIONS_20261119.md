## Corrections to Claude's Round 2 response

1. **F1 is not "untested representation" in general.** A real source-disjoint 18-label study [Action #44114903070](https://github.com/kolihhan/name-kaggle/actions/runs/44114903070) compared exact same-row char-TFIDF+LinearSVC `0.213704` against frozen MiniLM-L6-v2 + same head `0.107407`, **−0.106297 BA**, 169 rescues / 456 regressions, all 3 heldout sources negative. A separately executed frozen-geometry study [Result](../candidates/pretrained-source-geometry-v1/RESULT.md) on the older official prompt-group OOF gave SimCSE+linear `0.234860`, LUSR+linear `0.351198`, versus original own LoRA24 hard-OOF anchor `0.546761`. These are **different datasets and baselines**, so don't concatenate into one numerical trajectory. **What remains untested is specifically autoregressive decoder multi-layer representations with prompt-nuisance removal**, not generic dense embeddings.
2. **False denominator and unsupported cap:** Claude said the typography contradictions involved `23 TRAIN rows=0.85%` and an absolute BA ceiling `+0.009`. The measured `23/216=10.65%` are **spent Oleg216 external** true-label exclusions, **not TRAIN**, and are potential exclusions, **not observed changed predictions** in the 0.702 system. Can't divide these 23 by 2,699 or infer a Public gain bound. Demoting tiny heuristic patches is defensible; that numerical rationale is invalid.
3. **Don't rerandomize 150 prompt groups into imaginary new test data.** `5-fold × 3 repeats` on the same 150 previously studied official prompts does not produce a fresh independent holdout. Pre-register one original grouped 5-fold or a fixed grouping; compare exactly the same out-of-fold rows and prompt-cluster bootstrap. If doing k/C/model selection, nest selection strictly inside each training fold, or ideally **pre-freeze one backbone and two ablations** to avoid consuming folds as an adaptive hyperparameter search.
4. **F1 control must be meaningful.** A +0.08 BA gain over char-TFIDF alone doesn't prove competitiveness: old LoRA24 grouped OOF `0.5468` dwarfed frozen LUSR `0.3512`; current third-party champion Public 0.702 has no honest on-TRAIN OOF. Compare same-row, same-fold char baseline and available original LoRA24 OOF anchor **only if exact fold/lineage match and upstream exposure are verified**. Otherwise explicitly leave champion paired comparison unavailable, and don't claim blending weights.
5. **Prompt PCA projection is a separate tested hypothesis, not a free improvement.** Within a near-perfect prompt×model training Cartesian product, averaging all 18 model responses per prompt can remove much model signal already; projection may erase real model-identity axes. Fit scaler, prompt means, PCA and logistic readout **on training-fold rows only**. Center on fold-training mean before projection, and do not project heldout prompt means. Pre-freeze k=0 baseline and k=8 single intervention; k=32 only in an entirely new registered experiment.
6. **Strong hidden-state extraction controls:** `Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B-Base` is publicly listed Apache-2.0 and is available as an off-the-shelf decoder backbone (verify exact revision/license before use). It is **not** a served-weight match for the contest class `qwen3.8-max`, so interpret it only as an encoder. Pin model revision, tokenizer revision, transformers/torch versions, sequence truncation, layer indices, attention-mask pooling, pad-token treatment, dtype/shape/row ordering, no train-data download to public repo. T4 runtime estimates in Claude's response are unmeasured.
7. **Effect power/GO gate:** `+0.08` and bootstrap lower `+0.04` is an ambitious *mechanistic advance* gate, not a calibrated probability that hidden Public improves. Report paired 18-label BA and prompt-cluster CI, correct/regress by class/provider, effective prompt-group N and near-prompt lexical confounding; reduce asymptotic confidence on repeated, adaptively studied folds.
8. **Training remains allowed.** Official TRAIN-only grouped DEV experiments and owner-authorized public-source Colab T4 training are allowed under RULES.md. New perfect 18-label external panel is NOT required to start. However no exploratory Kaggle GPU, no paid APIs or data leaks, no automatic official submission based on CI.

No 0.702 OOF available.
