Use the public ATLAS source-conditioned Qwen3-8B asset only if its 18 labels, checkpoint, base and rights are admitted. Hypothesis: local identity evidence persists across segments but document mean likelihood is contaminated by high-influence spans. Alternative: robust segment summaries remove useful rare identity evidence, or model source bias dominates both. Fixed control: source with highest masked document mean logp. Fixed candidate: mean across scales of median contiguous-block relative logp; scales 2,4,8 only if >=8 tokens per block; <16 tokens excluded and coverage reported. No threshold/weight fitting. Per-token shared difficulty cancels, but source-specific offsets do NOT; global centering cannot be claimed to fix calibration. Real model execution requires admitted full18 cohort with independently established provenance/rights and GPU authorization or measured feasible CPU runtime. No prediction of .760 or submission until independent actual full18 comparison and runtime gate; preserve .718.
